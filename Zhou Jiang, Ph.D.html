Cell therapy durability

Stem-like CAR-T cells that persist

Can a T cell's stemness program be engineered directly, so CAR-T cells last longer in solid tumors?

Manuscript in preparation
2 MD Anderson invention disclosures

  • Engineered a TCF7-centered stemness lever that yields stem-like CAR-T cells with enhanced persistence and anti-tumor efficacy in vivo.
  • Designed and built CAR constructs against CD19, TROP2 and B7-H3 from scratch.
  • Mapped the epigenetic and proteomic state behind response with ATAC-seq and IP-mass spectrometry, identifying markers of T cell quality.
  • Benchmarked the program against published CAR-T clinical response datasets.

Why it matters. The lever is target-agnostic, so it can be added to CARs against different antigens to address durability, a common failure point for cell therapy in solid tumors.

Checkpoint resistance

Tyro3 and resistance to PD-1 blockade

Why do some tumors stay resistant to anti-PD-1/PD-L1, and can a tumor-intrinsic kinase explain it?

First author, J Clin Invest 2021
Cited 360 times

  • Identified the receptor tyrosine kinase Tyro3 as a driver of anti-PD-1/PD-L1 resistance that limits innate immunity and tumoral ferroptosis.
  • Showed in vivo that Tyro3 inhibition restores sensitivity, taking the target from discovery through pharmacological proof of concept.
  • Led the program as the AACR-Pfizer Immuno-Oncology Fellow, with milestone updates to Pfizer R&D leadership.

Why it matters. It positions Tyro3 as a candidate partner for checkpoint blockade, with a mechanism that links survival signaling in the tumor to failure of the immune response.

Metastatic niche

B7-H3 in breast cancer brain metastasis

How does B7-H3 shape T cell dysfunction and exclusion in triple-negative breast cancer that has spread to the brain?

Manuscript in preparation

  • Characterized B7-H3 as a regulator of T cell dysfunction, T cell exclusion and stromal immunosuppression in TNBC brain metastasis.
  • Mapped the niche with Xenium spatial transcriptomics, COMET multiplex imaging and high-parameter flow cytometry.
  • Built a dual-mechanism therapeutic hypothesis supported by spatial and in vitro validation.

Why it matters. Brain metastases respond poorly to current immunotherapy; spatial data show where T cells are excluded and which signals to target.
